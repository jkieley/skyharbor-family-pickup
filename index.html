From the road to the welcome

  1. Follow Terminal 4 → Parking/Garage.

    On Sky Harbor Boulevard, follow the garage signs and take the parking entrance ramp.

  2. Use regular self-parking on Levels 4–9.

    The garage is directly above the terminal. Park near the B-zone elevators if convenient; other zones also connect to the terminal.

  3. Photograph your parking location.

    Capture the level, zone letter, and space so you can find the car easily with luggage.

  4. Take the elevator down to Level 3.

    Follow International Arrivals signs near Security Checkpoint B. You can enter this public area without a boarding pass or going through TSA.
